AI-Powered Virtual Patient

2025–2027

Empathy-driven simulation for traumatic brain injury coach training. An AI-powered virtual patient environment designed to help rehabilitation coaches practice problem-solving training, interpersonal communication, and empathy in authentic simulated encounters. The system combines virtual patient personas, conversational interaction, structured instructional scenarios, and performance feedback to provide a safe and repeatable practice environment.

Electronic Problem-Solving Training — ePST

2024–2026

Mobile health learning for traumatic brain injury rehabilitation. ePST is an evidence-based microlearning intervention designed to support cognitive and psychological rehabilitation among adults with traumatic brain injury through structured problem-solving training. I led instructional design and contributed to usability testing, empathy interviews, recruitment, learning analytics, and formative and summative evaluation.

Reframe through EEG Biofeedback & Generative Art

Reframe through EGG

2026–2027

An interactive human–AI system that combines real-time EEG signals and participant voice prompts to generate and iteratively revise personalized artwork representing an individual's stress experience. Rather than using EEG to classify emotions, the system treats physiological signals as an expressive input that influences the visual generation process while participants remain actively involved in directing and interpreting the artwork.

Project PHoENIX

2021–2022

Participatory and inclusive extended-reality design. Project PHoENIX investigated autistic adults' experiences, preferences, and needs in extended reality and explored how immersive technologies could be designed more inclusively. I contributed to instructional design, session development, 3D modeling, environment construction, usability research, qualitative coding, and computational analysis.

St. Jude Gaze Analytics AOI Transitions & Visual Navigation

St. Jude Multilevel Gaze Analytics

A learning analytics project investigating how learners visually navigate and interact with a GenAI-enhanced online biomedical learning environment. Using eye-tracking data, I model learner behavior across both interface elements and slides through AOI transitions, revisits, fixation patterns, gaze dynamics, and navigation trajectories to identify patterns that can inform learning-environment usability and design.

Diabetes Journey

A learning analytics project examining how adolescents interact with a mobile health learning platform for Type 1 diabetes self-management. My work focuses on validating and analyzing platform usage data to characterize engagement patterns across learning modules and connect behavioral traces with the design and evaluation of the intervention.

Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting Scaffold-GS & Adaptive Capacity

Learning to Grow Experts: Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting

A computer vision project investigating how Mixture-of-Experts structures can adapt to spatially uneven scene complexity in anchor-based 3D Gaussian Splatting. Building on Scaffold-GS-style representations, the approach dynamically organizes and grows specialized experts so that model capacity can be allocated according to differences in local scene structure rather than relying on a uniform representation across the entire scene.

MoCE Framework Continual Vision-Language Learning

MoCE: Mixture of Communitified Experts

A continual vision-language learning framework designed to handle uneven task complexity across incremental learning stages. Built on a CLIP vision-language backbone, MoCE organizes specialized experts to allocate model capacity according to changing task demands, helping preserve previously learned knowledge while adapting efficiently to new classes and varying levels of task complexity.
