Hi, I'm Yueqi.

Researcher, Designer, & AI Developer

I design and study human-centered AI and digital learning experiences at the intersection of learning sciences, healthcare, and emerging technologies. My research examines how AI-powered simulations, learning analytics, and interactive technologies can support meaningful learning, rehabilitation, and human-AI collaboration.

My current doctoral research focuses on designing an AI-powered, empathy-driven virtual patient to prepare rehabilitation coaches to deliver problem-solving training for adults with traumatic brain injury. Alongside this work, I investigate learner interaction through eye tracking, generative AI, extended reality, and computational learning analytics.
